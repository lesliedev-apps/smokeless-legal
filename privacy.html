Privacy Policy

Smokeless – Quit Smoking • Markó László

1. Introduction

Welcome to Smokeless ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • User-Provided Data: Information you enter into the app, such as your username and habit tracking logs which is stored locally on your device.
  • Authentication Data: Account credentials or identifiers managed securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Device and Diagnostic Data: Automatic collection of crash logs, performance metrics, and device information (such as operating system and hardware identifiers) via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Purchase Data: Subscription statuses and transaction tokens processed securely through RevenueCat and the respective platform app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play Console).
  • AI Interaction Data (Optional): If you explicitly opt-in to use our AI-powered summary features, specific habit metrics are transmitted securely to the Google Gemini API to generate personalized insights.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and execute the core habit-tracking and financial savings calculations.
  • To manage subscriptions and verify active purchases.
  • To monitor and fix technical crashes, ensuring app stability.
  • To process optional AI analyses only when triggered by your direct consent.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data. We utilize trusted third-party service providers that process data under strict compliance standards:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): For backend authentication and crash reporting.
  • RevenueCat: For subscription infrastructure and receipt validation.
  • Google Gemini API: For optional artificial intelligence text generation based on your logs.

Third-Party Privacy Policies: Firebase, RevenueCat, Google General, Google Dev Terms, Google Dev Privacy

5. International Data Transfers

Because our infrastructure partners operate globally, your information may be transferred to and processed on servers located in the United States. All international data transfers from the EU or UK comply with legal frameworks utilizing the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and certified data privacy frameworks.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ technical and organizational security measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your data. You retain full control over your data: you can clear local records or execute a complete account and data reset directly through the application's settings menu at any time.

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access, update, or delete the data we hold about you.
  • The right to withdraw consent for optional data processing features (such as AI integration) instantly within the app.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected by updating the effective date of the policy within the app or on our hosting page.

9. Contact Us
