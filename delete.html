1. Deleting Your Data In-App

You have full control over your data and can completely remove all local tracking records and online account information directly within the Smokeless application at any time by following these steps:

  • Open the Smokeless app on your device.
  • Tap the avatar circle located in the top-left cornerstrong> of the main screen to open the Settingsstrong> menu.
  • Scroll down to the bottom of the settings page and tap the red button with a trash can icon buttonstrong> to execute a complete data and account reset.

This action permanently deletes all associated local databases, preferences, and online server-side data immediately.
